# Phasers & recipes

Phasers are multi-step attribute playback (absolute/relative layers, speed, phase, …). From MA ≥ 2.4, **PhaserRecipe** objects (violet recipe lines) hold steps and value sources on one object — **not** the same as StandardRecipe (`HRecipe`).

Automation/API detail: [`../phaser-recipe.md`](../phaser-recipe.md) (`source: mixed`). Product notes also in release-notes for 2.4+.
